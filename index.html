Print & Assembly

From filament
to power

STEP 01

Prepare the Print

Load black or dark grey PLA. Orient the throne upright as provided in the files.

STEP 02

Add Supports

Enable tree supports. The intertwined swords need careful support under the blades and seat.

STEP 03

Print the Throne

0.2 mm layers, 15–20% infill, 3–4 walls. Expect 12–18 hours depending on your printer.

STEP 04

Remove Supports

Carefully break away tree supports. Light sanding removes any remaining marks on the blades.

STEP 05

Optional Paint

Dry-brush the swords with silver or metallic paint for a battle-worn iron look.

STEP 06

Seat the Controller

Place your DualSense into the throne. It sits securely and looks ready to rule the Seven Kingdoms.

Print Settings & Tips

Forge a
clean result

Material

Black or dark grey PLA looks most authentic. PETG works too if you want extra durability. Silk or metallic filament adds extra sword shine.

Supports

Tree supports are strongly recommended. They are easier to remove from the thin sword blades without damaging the model.

Infill & Walls

15–20% infill and 3–4 walls give a solid, stable base that won’t tip when the controller is seated.

Post-processing

Light sanding + optional silver dry-brush turns the plastic into convincing iron. A matte clear coat helps protect the finish.
